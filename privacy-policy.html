It also describes how we may process Personal Information on behalf of companies we offer digital marketing services to (our "Services").

02

Data Collected

We process information that could identify, reasonably describe, or be associated with you ("Personal Information"). We may collect Personal Information directly when you visit our Site or interact with us, and may also receive it as part of the Services we offer to Clients.

  • When you visit our Site, we automatically collect information about your device, web browser, IP address, pages visited, and how you interact with the Site.
  • When we communicate with you, we may collect text messages, emails, phone calls, or audio messages. We may retain these for quality assurance and training.
  • On behalf of Clients, we may collect personal identifiers, demographics, device identifiers, internet and network activity, location data, commercial information, or preference data.
  • We may also collect de-identified, aggregate, or anonymised data that does not individually identify you.
03

Use of Information

We use the Personal Information we collect for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Running day-to-day operations, managing accounts, and communicating with you.
  • Processing and maintaining Personal Information on behalf of Clients as part of our Services — including digital advertising, Meta advertising, creative production, landing pages, and email/SMS campaigns.
  • Analytics on the effectiveness of marketing campaigns, advertisements, or conversion rates — including through our partnership with Varos, where applicable.
  • Our own marketing or advertising purposes, such as sending you relevant offers or insights by email.
04

Sharing Your Personal Information

We may share your Personal Information with vendors, service providers, or other third parties where necessary:

  • With third parties who perform services on our behalf, such as data analysis, email and hosting, or customer support.
  • With business partners and other brands when permitted by law or with your consent.
  • With third-party consultants or analytics providers to evaluate and improve our Site and Services.
  • With your consent or at your specific request.
05

How We Use Cookies

  • Security & functionality: Cookies help us run the site securely and enable basic functions like page navigation and session management.
  • Analytics: Cookies help us analyse how you interact with our Site, enabling us to monitor and improve performance and your experience.
  • Advertising: Cookies allow us and our advertising partners to deliver relevant ads, build interest profiles, and measure ad effectiveness across sites.

We are not responsible for the completeness or accuracy of any third-party opt-out options or programs. Our Site does not currently recognise "Do Not Track" signals, as there is no industry-wide standard for doing so.
